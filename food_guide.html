Food Guide 🍗

Healthy Diet for Dogs & Cats

Proper nutrition ensures long life, strong immunity, healthy coat, and overall well-being. Here is your complete food guide.

General Diet Tips

  • Fresh water must always be available
  • Avoid spices, oils, and processed foods
  • Introduce new foods slowly
  • Feed at consistent times

Dog Diet

Puppies

  • 3–4 meals/day
  • High protein kibble
  • Chicken rice, eggs, curd rice

Adult Dogs

  • 2 meals/day
  • Balanced carbs + meat + veggies

Senior Dogs

  • Low-fat meals
  • Joint supplements recommended

Cat Diet

Kittens

  • 3–4 small meals/day
  • High animal-protein diet

Adult Cats

  • Wet food preferred
  • Avoid milk (digestive issues)

Foods to Avoid

  • Chocolate
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Grapes

Daily Meal Plan

Morning: Kibble or chicken rice

Evening: Egg, fish meal, or meat bowl
