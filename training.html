Training Help 🎾

Train Smarter, Bond Better

Simple training techniques to help your pet obey commands and improve behavior.

Basic Commands

  • Sit
  • Stay
  • Come
  • Down
  • Leave it

Potty Training

  • Create a fixed potty area
  • Reward immediately after success
  • Maintain routine

Behavior Training

  • Discourage biting gently
  • Avoid shouting
  • Redirect bad behavior
