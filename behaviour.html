Track Your Pet’s Behavior

Monitor mood changes and behavior patterns.

Common Behaviours

  • Eating less / more
  • Aggression or growling
  • Over-sleeping
  • Fear or shaking
  • Hyperactivity

What To Do

  • Check for stress triggers
  • Provide calm environment
  • Consult vet if behaviour is unusual
