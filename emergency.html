Emergency Help For Your Pet

Quick actions can save your pet’s life. Stay calm and follow these steps.

What To Do During Emergencies

🐾 1. Bleeding / Injury

  • Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth.
  • If blood soaks through, don’t remove — add more layers.
  • Keep pet calm and restrict movement.
  • Visit a vet immediately.

🧪 2. Poisoning

  • Check what your pet consumed.
  • Do NOT make them vomit unless vet says so.
  • Bring the sample to the vet.

🥵 3. Heatstroke

  • Move pet to shaded or cool area immediately.
  • Apply cool (not ice) water on body.
  • Offer small amounts of water.

😿 4. Breathing Problems

  • Clear mouth of any blockage.
  • Keep neck straight.
  • Avoid touching chest too much.

🐍 5. Snake Bite

  • Keep pet still — movement spreads venom faster.
  • Do NOT try sucking venom.
  • Take to vet ASAP (golden hour = first 30 minutes).

⚡ 6. Seizures

  • Clear area around pet.
  • Do NOT hold the pet during seizure.
  • Record time duration.
