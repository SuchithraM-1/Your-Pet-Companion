Grooming Tips ✂️

Keep Your Pet Clean & Healthy

Good grooming prevents infections, maintains hygiene, and keeps your pet comfortable.

Bathing

  • Dogs: Every 2–4 weeks depending on breed
  • Cats: Self-cleaning, bathe only if dirty
  • Use pet-safe shampoo only
  • Dry completely to avoid fungal issues

Nail Trimming

  • Trim every 3–4 weeks
  • Use pet-safe nail clippers
  • Avoid cutting the “quick"

Ear Cleaning

  • Check weekly for wax, redness, or smell
  • Never use water inside ears
  • Use vet-approved ear solutions

Coat & Hair Care

  • Brush daily for shedding breeds
  • Long haired pets need trimming
  • Check for ticks & fleas regularly
